# Photo guide

Aim for roughly 20–30 clear photos of the same person. Use a mix of front, three-quarter, and side views, including left and right profiles where available. A few photos from slightly above or below eye level, and a mix of close, half-body, and full-body framing, can help show the subject in more than one setting.

Include some natural variation in expression, background, lighting, and clothing. Avoid making every image the same pose, outfit, or scene. Keep the person's face visible and in focus in most photos; remove images that are very blurry, heavily obstructed, or show a different person as the main subject.

These are practical suggestions rather than strict biometric or dataset requirements. The pipeline accepts a ZIP archive or a directory of captioned images; use the request schema and example for the expected path field.
